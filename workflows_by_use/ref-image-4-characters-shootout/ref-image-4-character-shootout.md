test:

I did a shootout around 6th July 2026 between Bernini, Licon MSR and Ingredients Lora (for LTX2.3).
I didnt put huge effort into success, but wanted to quickly see if they could handle:

4 character ref images and a location image.

I used the 4 characters from my "Black Magic" dialogue scene where they are waiting outside the lift, which I have already done using LTX 2.3 (FF, LF), and that can be seen along with the other scene related videos on dialogue here - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l8UHgQRXOcM

I'll post a video on this shootout results of the workflows to my YT channel https://www.youtube.com/@markdkberry

Conclusion:

Licon and Ingredients are okay but have weaknesses. 
Licon adhered to the prompt better but lost it when characters turned or left shot and there was a lot of bleed.
Ingredients lora was good for clothing, not so good for faces and hair, also has limit on size it can go to and I felt it was weak structurally but maybe that could be addressed with more understanding. It did seem to maintain seperation though.
Bernini I prefer, its just easier to use and you dont have to faff around giving it the right kind of ref images, though it did lose character consistency it stuck with what it gave. I also had to go to 1440 or something large for it to do a good job, but the prompt adherance felt stronger, but then WAN based models usually are.

I dont like any of the results, I'll be honest, but my ideal would be using Bernini then upscaling the result in LTX, but currently this isnt possible without FF+LF to control characters, and that wont work if they arent in the FF and LF shot... I am working on solving this issue.

FINAL DECISION:

image editing is still king. making FF and LF is the only way currently that will result in fidelity and high resolution final video clip for four characters (when you shove it through more processes to sharpen it up and upscale it) and even then you'll end up with problems if they turn around or leave shot.
