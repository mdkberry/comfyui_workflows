### Legal & IP Compliance Checklist for Celebrity AI Parody

You can save this checklist to ensure your content stays firmly within the legal boundaries of satire and fair use (USA):

- **Maintain "Transformative" Plotlines:** Ensure the celebrities are placed in fictional, surreal scenarios (like your VR simulation theme) to critique society or technology. The narrative must create an original artistic work, not a simple copy of their existing media.

- **Keep Visuals Intentionally Low-Quality:** Maintain a stylized, obviously non-realistic animation style. This serves as an immediate visual signal that the content is a parody, defeating claims of "deceptive intent" or fraud.

- **Use Intentionally Incorrect Voices**: Avoid perfect voice cloning. Using clearly wrong voices eliminates the risk of violating deepfake identity theft laws (like the US ELVIS Act) and reinforces the satirical nature of the project.

- **Pass the "No Reasonable Person" Test:** The script, visuals, and audio must be exaggerated enough that an ordinary viewer would understand it is a work of fiction, protecting you against defamation (libel/slander) claims.

- **Enforce Zero Direct Monetisation:** Do not charge viewers, use paywalls, or run ads on the videos. Keeping the project entirely free strips celebrities of the argument that you are commercially exploiting their "Right of Publicity" to make money.

- **Display a Permanent On-Screen Disclaimer:** Burn a visible text disclaimer directly into the video frame (e.g., "This is an AI-generated parody. All voices, likenesses, and VR settings are entirely fictional and intended as satire.").

- **Write Transparent Metadata:** Clearly label your video titles and descriptions on platforms like Odysee with phrases like "An Original AI Satire" or "Fictional VR Parody." Never use misleading titles that suggest the footage is real or leaked.
