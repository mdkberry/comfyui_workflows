## The AI Models Used For "Black Magic" Music Video

I began work on this video using ComfyUI in June 2026 with the LTX model and didn't get very far.

Then I switched to Bernini when that model came out. However, I struggled with it due to the XXX nature and how to portray the sensuality of the story, without it becoming smutt *(I go into more detail in the [YouTube linked video](https://youtu.be/822Zt8-QJ9U?si=pbtGTE8fQ1jmoF1P))*.

```
Family_A - Empty Stage
Family_B - Chorus_Engine
Family_D - BLONDE
Family_E - Flamenco woman
Family_F - Living Paintings
Family_J - Candlelight-ritual
Family_N - JACK
Family_P - Ballerina
Family_Q - Orchestra
Family_R - Glue_Shots
Family_X - Tantra Yoga
```

I usually develop scripts shot-by-shot, but with this one I had to do it using "families of themes" shown above, and that helped me access the story in the way I wanted to. I made lots of shots by theme until I found what I felt worked, and began to put it together like a jigsaw. Once the first complete draft was finalised, I saved the shots I was using into AIMMS using a shot-by-shot naming convention. *([AIMMS is my storyboard management software and is available from the website](https://markdkberry.com/software/))* 

I finally finished it *(end of August 2026)* using Minimax H3 pushing the original Bernini results through where they worked, and upscaling using to MMH3 as well as fixing with it. The workflows used are provided.

Then finally *(Oct 2026)*  throwing a lot of Davinci Resolve FX at it to strip away the clean aesthetic and give it some character *(probably a horror show to professional colourists, but I like it)*.

I'll do a video on the process I took at some point and will release that to the YT channel.
